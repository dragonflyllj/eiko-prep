Privacy Policy — Eikō Prep

Last updated: March 2026

Overview

Eikō Prep ("the App") is developed by 株式会社Prospect. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how the App handles data.

Data Collection

Eikō Prep does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The App operates entirely offline after the initial load.

  • No account or registration required
  • No personal information is collected
  • No data is sent to any server
  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs are used

Local Storage

Practice progress (stars and session counts) is stored locally on your device using browser localStorage. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser/app data.

Children's Privacy

The App is designed to be used by parents together with their children. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. No personal data of any kind is collected by the App.

Third-Party Services

The App loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) on first use. This is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. No other third-party services are used.
